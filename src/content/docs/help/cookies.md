---
title: How does Podlink use cookies?
---

Podlink uses a privacy-conscious cookie to enhance your listening experience by remembering your preferred podcast app.

## Setting a Preferred Podcast App

When you tap a podcast app on a Podlink page, you can choose to set it as your preferred app. Once selected:

- Future visits to Podlink pages from that browser will skip the interstitial page.
- You’ll be redirected automatically to your preferred app for a smoother experience.

## Opting Out of Automatic Redirection

Prefer to choose your app each time?

You can disable the auto-redirect by enabling the "Ask which app to use every time" toggle on the interstitial page banner.

> Note: On some mobile devices, especially with certain apps, you may be redirected before the toggle becomes visible. If that happens, visit the Podlink homepage and tap "Preferences" in the top right corner to manage your cookie settings.
