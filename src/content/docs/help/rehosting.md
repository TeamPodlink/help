---
title: Does Podlink cache or rehost my audio?
---

No. Podlink does not cache, rehost, or modify your podcast audio files in any way.

All episodes are streamed directly from the enclosure URL specified in your RSS feed. This means that:

- You maintain full control and ownership of your audio.
- All plays on Podlink are served from your original hosting provider.
- Your hosting analytics will accurately reflect Podlink listens.

If you're tracking downloads or streams, they’ll appear in your analytics just like plays from any other directory or player that respects your RSS enclosure.
