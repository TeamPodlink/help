---
title: Why can’t I find my podcast on Podlink?
---

If you’re having trouble finding your podcast on Podlink, here’s what you need to know:

## How Podlink Search Works

Podlink pulls podcast data from multiple sources:

- Apple Podcasts (via the iTunes Search API)
- Podcast Index
- Direct RSS feed URLs

If your podcast is listed in Apple Podcasts or Podcast Index, or if you have a valid RSS feed URL, it should be searchable on Podlink. There is no separate submission process to be included in our directory.

## What You Can Try

- Check your Apple Podcasts listing. If your podcast isn’t listed there, it may not appear in Podlink’s search results.
- Try searching by RSS feed URL. You can paste your full feed URL into the Podlink search bar.
- Verify the podcast title or author name. Make sure you’re using the same name that appears on Apple Podcasts or in your RSS metadata.

## Still Can’t Find It?

If your podcast is publicly available but still not showing up in Podlink search, please contact us with the following:

- Your podcast title
- A link to your Apple Podcasts listing or RSS feed
- Any additional details that might help us locate it

We’ll be happy to take a look.
