Podlink gives every podcast a single shareable link that helps listeners open the show or episode in the app they already use.

## What You Can Do With Podlink

- Share a podcast or a specific episode with one link.
- Let listeners choose their preferred podcast app.
- Route returning visitors directly to the app they use most.
- See Podlink-originated plays in your hosting analytics.
